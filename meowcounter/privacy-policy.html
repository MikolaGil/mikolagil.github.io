01 Overview

MeowCounter is a listening app: it counts how often your cat meows by analyzing the pitch of sounds picked up by your phone's microphone, entirely on the device itself. There is no cloud service, no speech-recognition API, and no server that MeowCounter talks to for the purpose of detecting meows. This policy explains what little data the app does handle, and why.

02 Microphone & audio

When you tap Listen, MeowCounter opens your microphone and runs a lightweight pitch-detection routine over the incoming audio in memory, in real time, entirely on your device.

  • Audio is never written to a file, never buffered beyond the fraction of a second needed to estimate pitch, and never recorded for playback.
  • Audio is never transmitted over the network, to us or to anyone else.
  • No cloud speech recognition, voice-print, or machine-learning service is used at any point — the entire detector runs locally.

What the app keeps is the result of that analysis — the moment a meow was detected — not the sound itself.

03 Data on your device

MeowCounter stores the following directly on your phone, using a local database and local preferences. None of it is uploaded, backed up to a server, or linked to an account, because MeowCounter has no accounts and no cloud sync.

  • Meow events — a timestamp each time a meow is detected, used to build your Statistics view.
  • Cat profile — name, age, and sex, only if you choose to fill these in under Settings.
  • Vet visit log — dates and notes you choose to add, if you use that feature.
  • App preferences — detection sensitivity and whether you've purchased Remove Ads.

04 Advertising

Unless you've purchased Remove Ads, MeowCounter shows banner and interstitial ads through Google AdMob (part of the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To select and measure ads, AdMob may collect identifiers such as your device's Advertising ID, IP-based general location, and app interaction data. This collection is governed by Google's own privacy practices, not MeowCounter's servers — we don't operate any ad infrastructure ourselves.

Read Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy, and its ad-specific terms at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

05 Purchases

The one-time Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. When you buy it, your payment details go directly to Google Play — MeowCounter never sees, collects, or stores your card number or billing address. We only receive confirmation that the purchase succeeded, which we store locally to unlock the Pro features on your device.

06 Permissions

MeowCounter requests the following Android permissions. Each exists to make listening for meows work reliably — none are used to gather data for any other purpose.

PermissionWhat it's for
MicrophoneRequired to detect meows — see "Microphone & audio" above.
Foreground serviceKeeps listening running reliably, including overnight, and shows a persistent notification while it does.
NotificationsDisplays that ongoing "listening" notification required by Android for foreground services.
Wake lockPrevents the CPU from sleeping mid-detection during a long listening session.
Ignore battery optimizationsOptional; stops Android from pausing the listening service to save power during long sessions.
Network accessUsed only to fetch and report on ads (see "Advertising") and to process purchases.

07 Children's privacy

MeowCounter is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app's own local data (meow counts, cat profile) never leaves the device regardless of who uses it.

08 Your controls

Because everything MeowCounter stores lives on your device, you're always in control of it:

  • Open Settings → Reset Data to permanently erase every meow count, cat profile field, and vet visit entry.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its local data along with it.
  • Revoke the microphone permission at any time from Android's app settings — MeowCounter simply won't be able to detect meows until it's granted again.

09 Changes to this policy

If MeowCounter's data practices change — for example, a new feature that handles data differently — this page will be updated and the effective date above will change accordingly. We encourage checking back occasionally if you'd like to stay current.
